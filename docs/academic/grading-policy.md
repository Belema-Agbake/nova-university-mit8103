# Academic grading policy

Nova uses a five-point scale: A (70–100, 5), B (60–69, 4), C (50–59, 3), D (45–49, 2), E (40–44, 1), and F (0–39, 0).

Quality points equal credit units multiplied by grade point. GPA and CGPA divide quality points by attempted units. Failed published attempts count in both totals. Draft, unpublished and incomplete results are excluded. Display rounding is limited to two decimal places; classification uses full-precision CGPA.

Retakes are additive historical attempts, never overwrites. For a course with multiple published attempts, the latest attempt is the active attempt for the current academic record; the original remains in the audit/history. The active attempt contributes once to GPA/CGPA, and a failed original remains visible as a historical carryover.
